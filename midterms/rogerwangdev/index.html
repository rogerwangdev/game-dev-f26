Targets & round rules
Choose your targets

Gold and diamonds earn money. Larger gold takes longer to haul. Rocks offer little value and use up time.

When does it end?

The round ends when time runs out or the field is cleared. Meet the goal to win. Treasure still being hauled when the round ends earns no money. Reaching the goal early lets you keep collecting.

Choose a target. Large gold is valuable but heavy. Rocks cost precious time.

What do the three recording groups mean?
Original checkpoint
The early October 26 test run. Gemini received the current game state without previous decisions. This recorded run scored $0/$750.
No history
Six final evaluation runs. Each Gemini request includes the current visible game state and legal actions, without previous decisions.
4 previous decisions
Six final evaluation runs with the same current state and legal actions, plus up to four previous decisions and their results from that episode. This is extra context, not training or memory across games.

All 12 final runs and the original checkpoint are available, including failures. Scores are recorded outcomes. Changing the selection starts its replay. Pause above the field to inspect the next recorded step.
