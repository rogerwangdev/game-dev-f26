## Your experience

- **One concrete moment:** I launched the claw mostly by feel and caught something. That was really satisfying and made me feel skilled. Later in the first round, I repeatedly missed the diamond, which became frustrating.
- **Clarity:** The goal and basic controls were familiar and easy to understand. It felt like a typical Gold Miner game. The main rule I was unsure about was what happens when the timer runs out while the claw is still extended or returning.
- **Enjoyment:** Timing a successful grab myself was rewarding. Watching the AI aim and launch so quickly and precisely felt almost like cheating: the task looked much less challenging for it, and less interesting to watch. I did not verify whether it had any unfair information advantage. The agent view also stuttered at times, although I could not tell whether that came from my connection, the playback, or the game.
- **One thing I or an agent would have to guess:** Whether a launch made before the countdown reaches zero can finish returning and still add an item's value to the score.
- **One question that removes that guess:** If I launch before the timer reaches zero, can the claw finish returning and add any collected item's value to my score, or does the round end immediately?
- **Most useful improvement and why:** Add difficulty options, with an optional aiming guide in a beginner mode or tutorial. This would help new players learn where the claw will go and make repeated misses less frustrating. A harder mode could leave the guide off to preserve the challenge of timing a shot by feel.
